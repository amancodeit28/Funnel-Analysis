# Funnel-Analysis
## Netflix Content Library: 

Netflix's catalog is a sprawling mix of movies and television, drawn from dozens of countries and spanning nearly a century of release years. This report explores a dataset of titles available on the service, looking at the balance between film and television, where content comes from, how long movies tend to run, how the library has grown over time, and how content is rated. Together, these views paint a clear picture of what fills the Netflix shelf.

### Movies Dominate the Library

The single biggest takeaway is that Netflix is, at its core, a movie platform. Of the 6,234 titles in the catalog, 4,265 are movies and just 1,969 are TV shows. That means roughly two out of every three titles are films, more than double the number of series available.

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Total titles | 6,234 |
| Movies | 4,265 |
| TV Shows | 1,969 |
| Countries | 73 |
| Ratings | 14 |
| Median movie duration | 98 minutes |

### A Truly Global Catalog, but Anchored in a Few Key Markets

Content on Netflix comes from 73 different countries, reflecting a genuinely international library. That said, a handful of markets supply the lion's share of titles. The top ten producing countries account for the bulk of the catalog, with the United States leading by a wide margin, followed by other major content hubs. This concentration shows that while Netflix reaches broadly, its content production is still rooted in a few dominant film and television industries.

### The Library Grew Sharply in Recent Years

Looking at when titles were released reveals a steep climb in recent years. For most of the twentieth century, only a small number of titles per year appear in the catalog. From the mid-2010s onward, however, both movie and TV show counts rise dramatically, with movies consistently outpacing television throughout the period. This pattern reflects how rapidly Netflix expanded its library as it grew into a global streaming leader.

### Movies Hover Around the Hour-and-a-Half Mark

Movie running times cluster tightly around a familiar feature-film length. The median movie runs 98 minutes, and the distribution shows most films falling within a predictable band around that figure, with a smaller number of much shorter or notably longer titles at the edges. In short, the typical Netflix movie is about an hour and a half long.

### Ratings Skew Toward Mature and Teen Audiences

The catalog spans 14 different content ratings, but the distribution is far from even. When broken down by content type, mature-audience and teen-oriented ratings carry the most weight, with movies and TV shows each contributing distinct patterns across the rating categories. This tells us the library leans toward older teen and adult viewers rather than being centered on younger-audience content.

### In Summary

The data describes a content library that is large, global, and rapidly expanded in recent years. Movies make up roughly two-thirds of the 6,234 titles and typically run about 98 minutes. Content arrives from 73 countries but is concentrated in a few leading markets, and ratings tilt toward mature and teen audiences. Taken together, these insights show Netflix as a movie-forward, internationally sourced platform that scaled its catalog dramatically as streaming took off.
